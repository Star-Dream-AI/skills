# Plain language: English and Chinese

These are working rules for public-service content, drawn from the U.S. Federal Plain Language
Guidelines, the European "Easy-to-Read" (Inclusion Europe) standard, the UK GOV.UK style guide and
Chinese simplified-writing practice for elderly and low-literacy readers (适老化、简明中文). Use the
level the user asks for. When they don't say, aim for **plain language** (about grade 6–8, or
something a primary-school graduate can read). Go to **easy read** (very short sentences, one idea
per line, icons optional) when the audience includes people with intellectual disabilities or very
low literacy.

## 1. Accuracy rules that override every style rule

Simplifying is where meaning gets lost. These come first:

- **Conditions keep their logic.** "A and B" must not become "A or B". "Only if" must not become
  "if". "Except", "unless" and "regardless of" must survive. If there are several conditions, use a
  list and say whether **all** or **any one** of them is needed.
- **Numbers stay exact.** Don't round, convert or average. "$2,610 a month" can't become "about
  $2,600". If a calculation would genuinely help readers (e.g. what 70% of ¥400 is), show it as
  marked help, mark it `derived` in the ledger and flag it for review.
- **Different deadlines stay different.** A postmark date, a received-by date and an in-person date
  are three facts. Write each one with what it applies to.
- **Hedges stay hedged.** "May", "subject to funds", "原则上" and "一般" carry real uncertainty. Keep
  it in plain words ("might", "usually, but not always") and flag it.
- **Don't add advice the source doesn't give.** You can say "call the number below if you are not
  sure". You can't invent a rule ("you will definitely qualify").
- **The official document wins.** Every output says so in its footer.

## 2. Structure

Readers scan. Put what they need to act on first:

1. What is this? (one sentence)
2. The most important things: deadline, money and the one action to take. Show them in a box.
3. Who can get it / who it's for
4. How much / what it costs (use tables for rows of numbers)
5. What to do, as numbered steps with one action each
6. What to bring
7. Important dates, written with weekday and year
8. Help and contacts
9. Where the information comes from (sources)

Use headings written as questions or tasks ("How do I apply?" / "怎么申请？"). Keep paragraphs to
1–3 sentences.

## 3. English rules

- Talk to the reader as "you". Call the agency "we" only if the agency itself is publishing. A
  volunteer guide says "the agency" or names it.
- Aim for 15–20 words per sentence at most, and write one idea per sentence.
- Use the active voice: "Send the form by…", not "The form must be submitted by…".
- Prefer common words: "use", not "utilize"; "before", not "prior to"; "must", not "shall".
- Write dates as `Tuesday, March 31, 2027`. Never use `3/31/27`, which is ambiguous outside the US.
- Write money as `$2,610 a month`. Spell out the period ("a month", "a year", "one-time").
- Explain each acronym on first use: "SNAP (food stamps)".
- Use bold for keywords only. No all caps, italics or underline (underline means a link).

## 4. 中文规则

- 用"您"称呼读者，句子要短，一句话只说一件事。每句最好不超过 25 个字，不要超过 40 个字。
- 多用主动句："请在 12 月 31 日前交费"，不说"缴费工作应于 12 月 31 日前完成"。
- 去掉公文套话：兹、特此、现将有关事项通知如下、予以、上述、凡……均……、按照有关规定。
- 金额、日期、电话一律用阿拉伯数字："400 元""2026 年 12 月 31 日（星期四）"。
- 写清"谁出钱"：个人交多少、政府补多少，不能让读者以为补助也要自己交。
- 第一次出现的专业词要解释：例如"低保（最低生活保障，给收入很低的家庭的补助）"。
- 条件用列表写，并说明是"全部符合"还是"符合其中一条"。
- "原则上""一般""视情况"不能删，要写成"一般是……，但也有例外，请先问清楚"，并标记待复核。
- 繁简：默认简体（`lang="zh-Hans"`）。读者在港澳台或海外繁体社区时用繁体（`lang="zh-Hant"`），并注意用词差异（"身份证"/"身分證"、"信息"/"資訊"）。

## 5. Translation between Chinese and English

Translation is the biggest risk. A plain-language rewrite and a translation together can drift far
from the source.

- Translate from the **ledger and the source**, not from your own simplified version. Then check
  that every fact value appears in each language.
- Keep official names in both languages the first time: "Minimum Living Allowance (低保 *dibao*)",
  "城乡居民基本医疗保险 (Urban and Rural Residents' Basic Medical Insurance)". Readers need the
  original term to talk to the office.
- Flag terms with no exact equivalent for review, e.g. 低保, 特困人员, 户籍, "qualified
  non-citizen", "categorically eligible", "public charge". Explain them; don't just transliterate.
- Currency: keep the source currency. Don't convert ¥ to $.
- Dates: convert the format only (2026年12月31日 → December 31, 2026), never the value.
- If the source is English and the reader reads Chinese, keep agency names, form names and phone
  numbers in English as well, so the reader can show them at the office: `申请表（Form HEAP-1）`.
- Mark the result as a translation in the footer: "本页根据英文原件翻译整理" / "Translated from the
  Chinese original".

## 6. Phrase tables

| Official English | Plain English |
|---|---|
| pursuant to / in accordance with | under, following |
| applicants shall submit | send |
| prior to / subsequent to | before / after |
| is required to | must |
| in the event that | if |
| eligibility determination | decision about whether you qualify |
| household gross monthly income | total money everyone in your home earns each month, before tax |
| recertification | renewing your benefits |
| subject to availability of funds | only while there is money left in the program |

| 公文表达 | 简明表达 |
|---|---|
| 现将有关事项通知如下 | （删掉，直接说事） |
| 参保缴费 | 交医保费 |
| 个人缴费标准为每人每年 400 元 | 每人每年交 400 元 |
| 财政补助 | 政府帮您出的钱（不用您交） |
| 予以资助 | 帮您出 |
| 待遇等待期 | 交费后要等一段时间才能报销 |
| 逾期不予受理 | 过了日期就不能办了 |
| 原则上 | 一般是……（有例外，请先问清楚） |
| 凡符合条件的 | 符合下面条件的人 |

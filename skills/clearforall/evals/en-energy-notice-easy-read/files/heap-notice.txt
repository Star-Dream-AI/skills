LAKEVIEW COUNTY COMMUNITY ACTION AGENCY
PUBLIC NOTICE
2026-2027 HOME ENERGY ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (HEAP)
Issued October 1, 2026

The Lakeview County Community Action Agency (hereinafter "the Agency") hereby gives notice that applications for the 2026-2027 Home Energy Assistance Program will be accepted from November 3, 2026 through March 31, 2027, or until available funds are exhausted, whichever occurs first. Pursuant to federal guidelines, HEAP provides a one-time payment to assist eligible low-income households with the cost of home heating.

SECTION 1. ELIGIBILITY

To be eligible, an applicant household must meet ALL of the following requirements:
(a) The applicant must reside in Lakeview County at the time of application;
(b) The household must be responsible for paying home heating costs, either directly to a utility or fuel vendor or as part of rent;
(c) At least one household member must be a U.S. citizen or qualified non-citizen; and
(d) The household's gross monthly income must not exceed the limit for its size as set forth in Table 1 below.

Households in which any member receives SNAP or SSI benefits are categorically eligible regardless of income, provided that requirements (a) through (c) are met.

Table 1. Maximum Gross Monthly Household Income
Household Size    Maximum Gross Monthly Income
1                 $2,610
2                 $3,413
3                 $4,216
4                 $5,019
5                 $5,822
6                 $6,625
For each additional household member, add $803.

Households residing in subsidized housing whose heating costs are included in their rent may be eligible for a reduced benefit.

Applicants who received a HEAP benefit during the 2025-2026 program year may be automatically enrolled and may not need to submit a new application. Such households will be notified by mail.

SECTION 2. BENEFIT AMOUNTS

Regular HEAP benefits range from $250 to $1,200 per household, based on income, household size, and primary heating fuel type. Benefits are paid directly to the household's utility company or fuel vendor and are not paid in cash to the applicant, except where heat is included in rent.

Emergency (crisis) benefits of up to $600 are available to eligible households that have received a written disconnection (shut-off) notice or have less than a 10-day supply of fuel. Crisis applications are processed within 2 business days of receipt of a complete application.

SECTION 3. HOW TO APPLY

Applications may be submitted in one of the following ways:
1. In person at the Agency office, 410 Harbor Road, Lakeview, Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. (closed on county holidays). Walk-in applications will be accepted until 4:00 p.m. on March 31, 2027.
2. By mail to: HEAP Unit, Lakeview County Community Action Agency, P.O. Box 2207, Lakeview. Mail-in applications must be received no later than March 13, 2027. Applications received after that date will not be processed.
3. Online at www.lakeviewcaa.example.org/heap.

For questions regarding your application, please contact the HEAP Unit at (555) 014-2290.

SECTION 4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION

Applicants must provide the following documents with the application. Incomplete applications will be denied.
- Photo identification for the applicant (driver's license, state ID card, or passport);
- Social Security numbers (or proof of qualified non-citizen status) for all household members;
- Proof of all household income received in the 30 days prior to the date of application (pay stubs, benefit award letters, or self-employment records);
- The most recent heating bill, or a statement from the landlord if heat is included in rent;
- Proof of Lakeview County address (lease, mortgage statement, or utility bill dated within the last 60 days).

SECTION 5. NOTICE OF DECISION AND RIGHT TO APPEAL

The Agency will mail a written decision within 30 days after receiving a complete application. Applicants who disagree with a decision have the right to request a fair hearing. Requests for a fair hearing must be made in writing within 60 days of the date on the decision notice.

SECTION 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS

All benefits are subject to the availability of federal funds. The Agency reserves the right to modify benefit amounts if program funding changes. Persons who knowingly provide false information may be required to repay benefits and may be subject to prosecution.

Language assistance and reasonable accommodations for persons with disabilities are available free of charge upon request. To request assistance, call the HEAP Unit at (555) 014-2209 or TTY 711.
